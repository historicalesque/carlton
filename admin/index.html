Tools

Review submissions

Entries sent in through the contribution form are listed in the team's submissions spreadsheet. Each row's Open link shows the entry and what it would change. Editors then accept or reject it in the same row.

Change a kept entry

An entry kept as its author wrote it has no contribution form, so no one else can suggest changes. To change it, or to open it up again by unticking Keep this entry as I wrote it, open it in the form here. What you send goes to the submissions spreadsheet like any other suggestion.

Edit directory and map data

Street directory and electoral roll records behind search, the maps and the People and Places pages. Take care: a mistake here affects every page of the site.
